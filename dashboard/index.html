Calibration & Brier Score Analysis

Brier score baseline measures the squared probability error. For 180-day retention, our model's Brier score of 0.2367 is slightly above the naive base rate baseline of 0.2096. This occurs because the time interval of donor returns in a synthetic dataset contains high variance. However, the 0.6160 ROC-AUC demonstrates the model successfully splits and prioritizes returning donors. For 365-day churn, the 0.7007 ROC-AUC demonstrates solid, strong discrimination signals, making it highly effective for targeted reactivation outreach.
