# Phase 2: Retention Drivers — Evidence Ranked

Sources: Transfusion, Vox Sanguinis, BMC Public Health, Asian J Transfusion Sci, Indian community studies, ISBT/WHO reports, Theory of Planned Behavior literature.

---

## Ranked Retention Drivers (Strongest Evidence First)

| Rank | Driver | Evidence Strength | Key Sources | Effect Direction |
|------|--------|-------------------|-------------|------------------|
| 1 | **Previous donation frequency** | ★★★★★ | Yeh 2009; Kauten 2021; Liu 2022 | More donations → higher return |
| 2 | **Time since last donation (recency)** | ★★★★★ | Yeh 2009 RFMTC; Yang 2020 RCT | Longer gap → lower return |
| 3 | **First-donation experience / habit formation** | ★★★★★ | Bagot 2016 SR; Callero 1999; Masser 2012 TPB | FT donors need distinct strategy; habit ~4th donation |
| 4 | **Inter-donation interval consistency** | ★★★★☆ | Liu 2022; UCI RFMTC | Regular intervals predict return |
| 5 | **Phone outreach for lapsed donors** | ★★★★☆ | Yang 2020 RCT (China); Indian tele-recruitment PMC 2014 | Phone > SMS for long-inactive |
| 6 | **SMS/text reminders (first-time & repeat)** | ★★★★☆ | TEXT study Transfusion 2020; Gemelli 2018 | +5–31% relative return lift for FT donors |
| 7 | **Temporary deferral handling** | ★★★★☆ | Marwaha 2012; IJH 2021 India review | Deferral without follow-up → dropout |
| 8 | **Donor satisfaction / staff interaction** | ★★★★☆ | Sachdeva 2023 India; Charbonneau 2016 | Poor experience → lapse |
| 9 | **Mobile donation camp access** | ★★★☆☆ | Srivastava 2025 South India; van Dongen 2015 | Camp convenience drives repeat in India |
| 10 | **Recognition / non-monetary incentives** | ★★★☆☆ | Malhotra 2026 North India; Grazzini 2021 | Recognition preferred over money in India |
| 11 | **Waiting time / process friction** | ★★★☆☆ | Charbonneau 2016; Shimla dropout study | Operational friction → dropout |
| 12 | **Seasonal / festival effects** | ★★★☆☆ | Marwaha 2015 NACO; Zimbabwe time-series | Oct–Dec camps peak in India |
| 13 | **WhatsApp / IMA communication** | ★★☆☆☆ | IAMR 2015; PubMed 2024 India survey; LMIC SR 2025 | High adoption; **mixed retention RCT evidence** |
| 14 | **Blood type demand signaling** | ★★☆☆☆ | Veldhuizen 2019 past-use appeals | Personalized need messages help |
| 15 | **Community engagement / donor clubs** | ★★★☆☆ | Shimla study; Zimbabwe pledge clubs | Red ribbon/life saver clubs improve retention |
| 16 | **Travel distance** | ★★★☆☆ | Shimla 2023 (>10 km dropout) | Distance barrier in India |
| 17 | **Corporate vs college camp type** | ★★☆☆☆ | Pune 2018; Mohammed 2025 | Segment-specific messaging |
| 18 | **Religious holiday scheduling** | ★★☆☆☆ | Anecdotal + seasonal data | Avoid major festival conflicts |

## Theory of Planned Behavior Mapping

| TPB Construct | Operational Proxy in System |
|---------------|-------------------------------|
| Attitude (altruism) | Camp participation, donation velocity |
| Subjective norms | Referral activity (not yet in data) |
| Perceived behavioral control | Walk-in ratio, deferral history |
| Intention → behavior | is_first_donation, gap consistency |

## Variables NOT Supported by Current Synthetic Data

- Waiting time minutes
- Deferral reason codes
- Reminder open/click rates
- Adverse reaction flags

These must be integrated from real blood bank CRM/clinical systems.
